## One routine for the whole unit: "Get to 1"
1. **Label** the two quantities and decide which one goes on top ("per what?" goes on the bottom).
2. **Get to 1:** divide both by the bottom number.
3. **Scale up:** multiply that one-unit amount by what the problem asks for.
4. **Sanity check:** bigger amount → bigger answer? Units make sense?
