# Unit 2 Study Guide — Proportional Relationships

Built from the 2.1–2.8 reference sheets and the Topic 2 Review. Every practice problem is original. Answer key at the end.

## The one idea
Two quantities are **proportional** when y is always the same number times x: **y = kx**.
**k** is the unit rate: how much y for exactly 1 x.

## Vocabulary (the review asks you to define these)
| Word | Means | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ratio | A comparison of two amounts | 3 cups : 2 cups |
| Rate | A ratio with different units | 90 miles in 2 hours |
| Unit rate | A rate "per 1" | 45 miles per 1 hour |
| Proportional relationship | y/x is the same for every pair | 2→6, 5→15 (always 3) |
| Constant of proportionality (k) | That same y/x value | k = 3 |
| Proportion | An equation of two equal ratios | 2/6 = 5/15 |

## Your toolkit: one move per lesson
| Lesson | The move | Say it to yourself |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1 | Divide to get to 1, then multiply to scale | "Per means divide. Get to 1." |
| 2.2 | Same, with fractions: keep, change, flip | "Flip the bottom, then cancel." |
| 2.3 | Divide y ÷ x on **every** row | "Every row, same number, or no." |
| 2.4 | k = y ÷ x, write y = kx | "k is y over x." |
| 2.5 | Straight line AND through (0, 0); read (1, k) | "Straight and through zero." |
| 2.6–2.7 | Decide first: times or plus? | "Times? Ratio. Plus? Just add." |
| 2.8 | Make the scale 1 inch, multiply; area uses real sides | "One inch first, then multiply." |

## Traps the test likes
1. Comparing totals instead of unit rates ("she made more shots").
2. A rate turned upside down (x/y instead of y/x).
3. A group price used as k ("3 lemons for $1.80" means k = 0.60, not 1.80).
4. A straight line that doesn't go through (0, 0), so it's NOT proportional.
5. One row in a table that breaks the pattern.
6. Ages, a starting fee, or "plus 1 per guest": NOT proportional.
7. Area on a scale drawing: convert each side first, never area × k.
8. Minutes inside an hour problem: 15 min = 1/4 h, 6 min = 1/10 h.

## Practice test

**2.1 Ratios, rates, unit rates**
1. Trail mix A is 3.5 lb for $10.15. Trail mix B is 4.25 lb for $11.90. Which is the better deal, and by how much per pound?
2. 7 notebooks cost $19.25. How much do 12 notebooks cost?

**2.2 Unit rates with fractions**
3. A turtle-bot rolls 5/8 mile in 1/4 hour. What is its speed in miles per hour?
4. There are 240 Calories in 2/3 cup of granola. How many Calories are in 3 cups?
5. A recipe uses 1 1/2 cups of flour for every 3/4 cup of sugar. How much flour goes with 3 cups of sugar?

**2.3 Equivalent ratios**
6. Is this proportional? Explain using numbers.
   | x | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | y | 10.5 | 21 | 31.5 | 43 |
7. This table is proportional. Find the missing x, then find y when x = 7.
   | x | 4 | 10 | ? |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | y | 6 | 15 | 27 |
8. Table: x = 8, 12, 20 and y = 2, 3, 5. Select ALL that are true.
   - (a) It's proportional. (b) y/x = 4. (c) When x = 28, y = 7. (d) When y = 1.5, x = 6. (e) When x = 16, y = 5.

**2.4 Constant of proportionality**
9. 9 gallons of gas cost $33.75. Write an equation for the cost y of x gallons. What do 14 gallons cost?
10. Which equations are proportional? y = 4x, y = 4x + 1, y = x + 4, y = 0.4x
11. For every 4 movie tickets, the cost goes up $26. Write y = kx. How many tickets can you buy with $91?

**2.5 Graphs**
12. A line goes through (0, 0) and (3, 12). Is it proportional? What is k? Name the point where x = 1.
13. A line starts at (0, 5) and goes through (2, 11). Is it proportional? Why or why not?
14. A graph of hours worked (x) vs. dollars earned (y) is proportional and passes through (6, 45). What does (6, 45) mean? What is k?

**2.6–2.7 Proportional reasoning**
15. Ben is 5 when his sister is 2. How old is Ben when his sister is 10? Can you use proportional reasoning? Explain.
16. Punch uses juice to soda in a 4 : 3 ratio. Last time you used 6 cups of soda. This time you're making 3 times as much punch. How much juice do you need?
17. A music app costs $12.50 per month. You were charged $175 for one year. Were you charged correctly? How many extra months were you charged for?
18. A bead kit should have 2 red beads for every 5 blue beads. A bag has 14 red and 30 blue. Give one way to fix the bag.

**2.8 Scale drawings**
19. Scale: 1 in. = 2.5 ft. A wall is 6.4 in. on the drawing. How long is the real wall?
20. A rectangle on a drawing is 3 in. by 5 in. Scale: 1 in. = 4 yd. What's the actual area?
21. A logo drawing is 10 in. long and 4 in. tall, at a scale of 1 in. = 3 ft. You redraw it 12 in. long. What's the new scale? How tall is the new drawing?
22. On a map, 3/4 in. = 5 miles. Two towns are 2 1/4 in. apart. How far apart are they really?

## Answer key (check after each section, not during)

1. A: $10.15 ÷ 3.5 = $2.90/lb. B: $11.90 ÷ 4.25 = $2.80/lb. **B is better by $0.10 per pound.**
2. $19.25 ÷ 7 = $2.75 each. × 12 = **$33.00**.
3. 5/8 ÷ 1/4 = 5/8 × 4 = **2 1/2 mph**.
4. 240 ÷ 2/3 = 360 Calories per cup. × 3 = **1,080 Calories**.
5. (3/2) ÷ (3/4) = 2 cups of flour per cup of sugar. × 3 = **6 cups**.
6. 10.5/3 = 3.5, 21/6 = 3.5, 31.5/9 = 3.5, 43/12 ≈ 3.58. **Not proportional:** the last row breaks the pattern.
7. k = 6/4 = 1.5. 27 ÷ 1.5 = **x = 18**. When x = 7, **y = 10.5**.
8. k = 2/8 = 0.25. **True: (a), (c), (d).** (b) is the flipped ratio. (e): 16 × 0.25 = 4, not 5.
9. k = 33.75 ÷ 9 = 3.75. **y = 3.75x.** 14 gallons: **$52.50**.
10. **y = 4x and y = 0.4x.** The others add a number.
11. k = 26 ÷ 4 = 6.5. **y = 6.5x.** 91 ÷ 6.5 = **14 tickets**.
12. Yes (straight, through the origin). **k = 12 ÷ 3 = 4.** The point is **(1, 4)**.
13. **Not proportional:** it's straight but doesn't pass through (0, 0). There's a starting amount of 5.
14. **$45 earned for 6 hours of work.** k = 45 ÷ 6 = **$7.50 per hour**.
15. **Not proportional:** Ben is always 3 years older (plus, not times). Sister 10 → **Ben 13**.
16. Last time: 6 ÷ 3 = 2 groups → 8 cups of juice. Three times as much: **24 cups of juice**.
17. 12 × $12.50 = $150. **Overcharged $25**, which is **2 months** ($25 ÷ $12.50).
18. 14 red ÷ 2 = 7 groups → needs 35 blue: **add 5 blue**. (Or 30 blue ÷ 5 = 6 groups → 12 red: remove 2 red.)
19. 6.4 × 2.5 = **16 ft**.
20. Real sides: 3 × 4 = 12 yd, 5 × 4 = 20 yd. Area = **240 sq yd**.
21. Real size: 30 ft long, 12 ft tall. New scale: 30 ft ÷ 12 in. = **1 in. = 2.5 ft**. New height: 12 ÷ 2.5 = **4.8 in**.
22. (9/4) ÷ (3/4) = 3, so 3 × 5 = **15 miles**.
